Moments Clip Studio privacy policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Moments Clip Studio is an internal publishing tool operated by Capysoft LLC. It posts short videos to Facebook Pages, Instagram accounts and YouTube channels that the studio owns, and reads the view and engagement counts of those posts. It is not offered to the public and has no end users other than the studio's own staff.

What we access

  • The names and IDs of the Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts the studio connects.
  • Access tokens that let the tool publish videos to those accounts.
  • Insights for posts the tool published: plays, reach, likes, comments, shares and watch time.

We do not read personal profiles, friends lists, messages, or data about people who view or comment on our posts beyond the totals above.

How we use and store it

The data is used only to schedule and publish the studio's videos and to measure how they perform. Tokens are stored encrypted on the studio's own servers. We do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are deleted when an account is disconnected. Post metrics are kept while the studio operates the account. To have any data deleted, email clipping@capyvisa.com or remove the app under Settings & privacy, Business integrations (Facebook) or Apps and websites (Instagram); see the data deletion instructions.
