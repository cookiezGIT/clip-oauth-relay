Data deletion instructions

Moments Clip Studio keeps only the access tokens and post metrics of accounts the studio connected itself. To delete them:

  1. Facebook: go to Settings & privacy, Settings, Business integrations, select Moments Clip Studio and choose Remove. Instagram: go to Settings, Apps and websites, and remove Moments Clip Studio.
  2. Email clipping@capyvisa.com with the account name and the words "delete my data". We delete the stored tokens and metrics within 30 days and confirm by email.

See also the privacy policy.
